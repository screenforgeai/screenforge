Take a screenshot from any app with Option+Shift+S on macOS (Alt+Shift+S on Windows and Linux): drag over an area, or press Enter for the whole screen, and it opens in the Screenshot editor. Option+Shift+R (Alt+Shift+R) opens the toolbar ready to record. The menu bar icon has a new Take Screenshot item, the toolbar's tooltips and the Keyboard Shortcuts dialog list both shortcuts, and arrow keys nudge a selected area (hold Shift to move it faster). If another app already uses one of these shortcuts, Screenforge leaves it alone and doesn't show it.
