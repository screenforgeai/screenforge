Area recordings now open the moment you stop, at any length. They used to be trimmed to the area first, which took about half the recording's length and failed after 15 seconds, so area recordings longer than about 45 seconds never opened. The editor now crops to exactly the area you selected, and you can adjust it afterwards in Frame. Auto zoom and the zoom position picker now aim correctly in any cropped recording.
