New installs now start with a short setup window before the editor. It welcomes you, asks for your name and email (optional, with a choice to get product updates and tips), and on macOS explains and asks for Screen Recording and Accessibility. When Screen Recording needs Screenforge to reopen, it does that for you and brings you back to the same step. Then pick your language and whether to share usage data, and start your first recording or look around the editor first. If FFmpeg is missing, it installs during the last step. If you already use Screenforge, you won't see it.
