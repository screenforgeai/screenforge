Share a link in one click. After an MP4 or WebM export, Share link uploads the video to Director and copies its link, with progress and Cancel. An upload the network or a quit interrupts resumes where it stopped. Recordings shows each project's link and when it expires, with Copy and Remove. Needs the optional Director sign-in; on the free plan links last 30 days (with an email 3 days before they expire), videos can be up to 10 minutes and you can have 25 active links, while paid plans keep links forever.
