'screenforge': patch

On Linux with Wayland, pressing Record now opens the system's share dialog straight away, and the countdown starts only after you pick a screen or window. Closing the dialog simply cancels. Area recording works the same way: pick the screen first, then draw the area on a still of it, and the recording is cropped to exactly that area on any monitor. The Window button no longer opens an empty list; its tooltip says you choose the app when recording starts.
