Use your Director workspace's brand kit as a style preset. When you're signed in to Director, the Preset menu shows a read-only "Brand kit" with the workspace logo, colours, background, cursor, caption style and font, and keeps it in sync; save it as a new preset to make your own changes. Presets can now also hold a logo (shown in a corner of the video, in the preview and in exports), the custom wallpaper image, and a font for captions and text. Add, move, resize or remove a logo in Frame › Logo.
