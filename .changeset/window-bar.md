Add a window bar: a Mac, Windows or browser-style bar drawn over the top of your recording. Pick a style under Window bar in the Frame panel (hover a style to preview it), choose its theme (Auto matches the page), and type what it shows (it starts with the recorded page's title; an address gets the lock icon). Frame's corners and shadow wrap the bar and the page as one window, and the bar zooms with it. Screenshots of a window can get the same bar, drawn sharp at 2x; cropped screenshots don't get one. When you record a browser window and turn the bar on, Screenforge looks for the browser's own toolbar and crops it off when it's sure, so the two bars don't stack. Otherwise it asks you to adjust the crop, and you can drag the crop line yourself or turn it off. Style presets now save the window bar too.
