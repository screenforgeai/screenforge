Add click sounds: pick Mouse, Trackpad, Soft, Pop or Tick from a new Click sound dropdown in the Cursor panel, with a play button on each option and a volume slider. Clicks play a press and, for Mouse, Trackpad and Soft, a release on mouse-up, in both the preview and exports. Projects that used the old click sound presets open with the closest new sound.
