Screenshots can now be shown in a lit 3D studio. Pick a Look in the Screenshot editor's Style panel (Night glow, Paper studio, Graphite stage, Warm sunset, Aurora, Rim light or Macro) and the screenshot sits on a thin glass panel with real reflections, a soft shadow on the backdrop and, for Macro, a close lens with depth of field. Drag the screenshot to move it, and drag the small light to change where the light comes from. Annotations are drawn straight onto the tilted screen, and Copy and Save export the finished, sharp image. Flat stays the default.
