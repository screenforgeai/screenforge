Screenshots now open in a lit 3D studio, seen through a macro lens: the screenshot sits on a thin glass panel with soft reflections, a shadow on the backdrop and a soft focus band. Everything happens on the canvas, with no settings panel: click to focus (it starts on the most detailed part of the screenshot), scroll over the focus ring to widen what stays sharp, drag to turn the camera, scroll to zoom, hold Space and drag or scroll to move the screenshot, and drag the sun to move the light (scroll over it for brightness). Double-click empty space to reset the view. Annotations are drawn straight onto the tilted screen, and Copy and Save export the finished image at full quality. Without 3D support (no WebGL 2), the editor falls back to the flat frame.
