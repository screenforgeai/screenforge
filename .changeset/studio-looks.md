Screenshots now open in a lit 3D studio, seen through a macro lens: the screenshot sits on a thin glass panel with soft reflections, a shadow on the backdrop and a soft focus band. Everything happens on the canvas: click to focus (it starts on the most detailed part of the screenshot), scroll over the focus ring to widen what stays sharp, drag to turn the camera, scroll to zoom, hold Space and drag or scroll to move the screenshot, and drag the sun to move the light (scroll over it for brightness). Hover the focus ring or the sun for a few more options: a wider or narrower sharp area, more or less blur, a brighter or dimmer light, and softer or crisper shadows. The Style sidebar keeps the other looks and the aspect ratio. Double-click empty space to reset the view. Annotations are drawn straight onto the tilted screen, and Copy and Save export the finished image at full quality. Without 3D support (no WebGL 2), the editor falls back to the flat frame.
