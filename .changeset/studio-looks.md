Add studio looks to the Screenshot editor: pick a Look in the Style panel and your screenshot sits on a thin glass panel in a lit studio, with soft reflections, a shadow on the backdrop and the screen's own glow. There are seven looks (Night glow, Paper studio, Graphite stage, Warm sunset, Aurora, Rim light and Macro, a close lens with a soft focus band), and Flat keeps the screenshot as it is today. Drag around the screenshot to turn the camera and scroll to zoom; hold Space and drag to move it anywhere in the frame, or Space and scroll to bring it closer or push it back; and drag the small light to change where the light comes from (double-click it to put it back). Annotations are drawn straight onto the tilted screen, and Copy and Save export the finished image at full quality. If your computer can't show 3D (no WebGL 2), the looks are turned off and the screenshot stays flat.
