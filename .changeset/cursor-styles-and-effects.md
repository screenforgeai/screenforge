The cursor now comes in eleven styles: macOS, macOS white, Windows, Touch, Wedge, Collaborator, Rounded, Gradient, Glitter, Glass and Sparkle. Collaborator and Rounded carry a name tag with your choice of name and colour. Clicks can show a Ripple, Pulse, Sparkle burst or Confetti, and "Shrink on click" gives each click a little press. Cursor effects add a Highlight, a Spotlight that fades the rest of the screen, a Magnifier, or a Glitter trail. Hover any option in the Cursor panel to preview it on your own recording. Rotation, motion blur, camera follow and easing have moved under Advanced. Existing projects open exactly as before.
